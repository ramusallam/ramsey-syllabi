Design for Social Good

Instructor Ramsey Musallam, EdD ramsey.musallam@sonomaacademy.org · Office hours: every Coyote Block, Room B220

Design for Social Good is a project-based class where students practice human-centered design thinking on a real need in the world. This semester the theme is assistive technology for computer access. Students build low-cost, open-source adaptive controllers, switches, and sensors that let an end-user operate a computer using whatever motion they can reliably make, at a fraction of the cost of commercial gear. The social good at the center is straightforward: assistive technology lets individuals with disabilities participate in society on the same terms as everyone else.

AI use

You may use AI engines (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, etc.) for tips, further explanations, and feedback, but not to produce or complete work you present as your own. Always check AI output for accuracy. For assessments and graded work, AI is only permitted when explicitly stated.

Given the nature of this course, AI is intentionally leveraged in a Vibe Coding capacity to supplement and bring to life student design prototypes and concepts. That use is built into the projects and is encouraged.

Materials

A laptop with reliable internet running Chrome or Edge (the Vibe Coding editor uploads to your Arduino over Web Serial, which only those browsers support). All prototyping materials (Arduino, switches, sensors, perfboard, solder, 3D-print filament, consumables) are provided in class.
